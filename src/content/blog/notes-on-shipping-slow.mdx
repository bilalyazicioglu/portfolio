"Move fast" is good advice until the fast path is the wrong path. I've shipped things quickly that I then spent three times as long unwinding.

## Where slow wins

1. **Data models.** Renaming a column is a migration. Renaming a concept is a rewrite. Spend the extra day here.
2. **Public APIs.** Once something is called from outside your codebase, every future change carries the cost of a breaking change.
3. **The first user-facing copy.** Placeholder text has a way of shipping to production. Write it like it's final.

## Where fast wins

Almost everything else. Internal tools, prototypes, anything behind a flag, anything you can delete in a day if it's wrong.

The skill isn't "move fast" or "move slow" — it's telling the two apart before you've committed to either.
